# SNUPI Slide Brief

Artifact: presentation slide or slide deck

Use `DESIGN.md` and match the SNUPI Lab Tech Indigo family.

## Required Structure

- 16:9 canvas.
- White or very light `#F7F8FF` background.
- Full-width top band and bottom band in `#4169E1`.
- Lower-right SNUPI logo area above or aligned to the footer band.
- Footer includes date and page number in white.
- Title at upper left with a vertical blue accent bar.

## Content Rules

- Use one large figure, equation, table, or diagram as the main visual object.
- Main object should occupy about two-thirds of the usable slide area.
- Title should be at least 35 pt.
- Body text should be at least 25 pt.
- Use no more than six lines of explanatory text.
- Prefer a short caption or TL;DR over paragraphs.

## Palette

- Main highlight: `#5B6CFF`.
- Existing slide bands: `#4169E1`.
- Supporting highlights: `#8E97FF`.
- Critical callout: `#A855F7`.
- Text and outlines: `#232833`.

## Avoid

- Crowded slide collages.
- Long prose blocks.
- Small legends or axis labels.
- Decorative gradients or heavy shadows.
