# SNUPI Paper Figure Brief

Artifact: paper figure, chart, or multi-panel research figure

Use `DESIGN.md` and prioritize publication readability.

## Figure Family

- Background: white by default.
- Text and axes: `#232833`.
- Subtle grids and panel borders: `#E7EAF4` or `#DDE3F1`.
- Primary method/result: `#5B6CFF`.
- Secondary comparison: `#8E97FF`.
- Critical callout: `#A855F7`.
- Baselines and controls: neutral muted gray.

## Typography

- Use Inter, Pretendard, Aptos, Arial, or a journal-compatible sans-serif.
- Keep labels readable after the figure is inserted into the final paper.
- Use consistent capitalization and units.
- Use direct labels when possible.

## Composition

- Align panels to a grid.
- Keep panel letters small and stable.
- Minimize legends.
- Avoid crowded multi-series plots.
- Use accent violet for one specific contrast, not for every decoration.

## Export

- Prefer vector output for diagrams and plots when possible.
- For raster outputs, export at high resolution with a transparent or white background as the target venue requires.
